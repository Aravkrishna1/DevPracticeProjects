A little about me

Design with
thought behind it.

I’m Alex Morgan, an independent designer and developer who enjoys making digital experiences feel clear, useful, and human.

Curiosity is part of the process.

I work across strategy, visual identity, and product design. My process starts by listening and asking the right questions, then finding the simplest way to make an idea real.

In recent years, I’ve partnered with early-stage teams and established brands to shape products and identities people want to spend time with. I like thoughtful details, open collaboration, and work that makes a practical difference.

When I’m away from the screen, you’ll probably find me exploring a new neighborhood, visiting a gallery, or trying to keep a houseplant alive.
